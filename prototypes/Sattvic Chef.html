Welcome, young chef!

Jain families follow a special sāttvic (pure) diet. Food is chosen so that the least possible harm comes to any living being — even tiny ones!

🥕 No root vegetablesPotato, onion & garlic are avoided — pulling them out kills the whole plant & soil life.
🍯 No honeyBees work hard for it — taking it harms them.
🍎 Above-ground foodsFruits, grains, dals & leafy greens that don't kill the plant are welcome.
🕉️ Anantkāy foodsFoods housing countless tiny beings (like figs) are avoided by careful Jains.

Drag each food into a basket — or just tap a basket. You'll learn why after every sort!
